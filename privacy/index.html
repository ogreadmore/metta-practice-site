Privacy

A small site with a small data footprint.

Effective July 25, 2026

This website is operated by Taylor Oliphant. Questions or data requests may be sent to taylor@aifreelancer.co.

Information collected

The site’s hosting provider may receive ordinary technical data such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, referrer, and timestamp in server logs. If you use the contact form or email directly, the name, email address, and message you provide are retained so the request can be answered.

Contact-form submissions are processed by Formspree, which sends the message to Taylor and may retain submission and technical data under its privacy policy. Contact-form details are used to answer the message, not to enroll the sender in a mailing list.

Purchases are completed through Lemon Squeezy. Lemon Squeezy processes checkout details, payment information, tax information, receipts, file delivery, refunds, and fraud prevention under its own privacy terms. This site does not receive or store full payment-card numbers.

How information is used

  • To operate and secure the website.
  • To fulfill purchases, provide support, and process refunds.
  • To answer messages and free-access requests.
  • To meet tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and legal obligations.

Marketing and analytics

Purchasing the book does not by itself enroll you in a separate marketing email series. The public site does not load advertising pixels at launch. If optional analytics or advertising tools are added, this policy and the site’s consent controls will be updated before they are activated where consent is required.

Retention and disclosure

Messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for support and records. Formspree retains submissions according to the service plan and its policies. Transaction records may be retained as required for accounting, tax, dispute, and legal purposes. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the site and fulfill a request, or when required by law.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information held directly by the operator. Some transaction records cannot be deleted immediately when retention is legally required. Lemon Squeezy account or checkout requests may also need to be directed to Lemon Squeezy.

Children and sensitive information

The site is not directed to children under 13. Please do not include medical details, trauma history, or other sensitive personal information in a free-access or support request; none is required.
