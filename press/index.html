The Science, History, and Art of Universal Love

Short description

Metta Practice is a concise, evidence-informed guide to loving-kindness mental training for people seeking less stress, better mood, less self-criticism, and more positive connection. It teaches one portable method in four movements—find it, strengthen it, expand it, return to it—while documenting the research and its limits.

Author

Taylor Oliphant has studied and practiced metta meditation for more than fifteen years. He lived in Nepal for three years, where he continued studying meditation. The book’s translation of the Metta Sutta is his own.

Interview topics

  • What the research on loving-kindness meditation supports—and what it does not.
  • Why universal love can be treated as a trainable orientation rather than a sentiment.
  • The Metta Sutta as a complete path and a poem for daily life.
  • How one real feeling of love can become the anchor for a larger practice.
  • What living in Nepal revealed about traditions that overlap in ordinary life.
  • Why the complete first edition is being given away without signup or payment.
