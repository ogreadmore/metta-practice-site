One practice. A lifetime of use.

Metta is the deliberate practice of goodwill. The book explains the science and history, then teaches a complete method you can bring into meditation, work, relationships, conflict, ambition, and ordinary life.

Taylor Oliphant has studied metta for more than fifteen years, including three years living in Nepal. He wrote this book to make the practice clear, credible, and genuinely useful.
